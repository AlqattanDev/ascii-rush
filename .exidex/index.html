ASCII RUSH

The last train left without you. Run the city after dark, ride the train roofs, bank the coins.

Drawn in text Swipe or keys Locker and bank One file, no deps

Every frame is characters on a canvas, projected with a real camera so the road has depth and the towers throw light and shadow. Three lanes, jumps, slides and ramps onto the train roofs; coins mark the safe line. Survive and the coins bank for skins and upgrades. The whole game is one HTML file with no dependencies, built to run at full speed on a landscape phone.

Swipe on a phone. WASD or arrows on a keyboard, Space to jump, P to pause. Progress saves on this device.
